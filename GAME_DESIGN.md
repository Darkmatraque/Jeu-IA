# Blackout Courier
Real-time twin-stick arcade combat, extraction roguelite. Core loop: move, aim, dodge, collect XP, select one of three upgrades, clear waves and decide extraction or endless. Districts: city 12 waves, reactor 16 with hazards, ground zero 20 with hazards and more enemies; unlock through successful extraction. Guardians every four waves, increasingly complex radial and targeted attacks. Three ships, three permanent upgrade trees, local records and achievements. No guaranteed popularity or playtime; further balancing requires players.
